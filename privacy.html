Privacy policy

VPS Drive Backup is a personal, single-user utility. It uses Google Drive's drive.file permission to create, list, read, update, and remove its own encrypted backup files in the owner's Google Drive.

VPS files are encrypted on the VPS with restic before upload. Google Drive stores the encrypted backup files and associated file metadata. The utility does not access unrelated Drive files, sell data, share data with other users, or use Drive data for advertising.

The owner controls the backup files in Google Drive and can remove them there. The owner can revoke this app's access in Google Account settings. Backups are retained according to the owner's configured retention schedule.

For questions, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
